## Heavy Metal Wheeljack 3D model

The wheeljack website uses a modified version of
[Heavy Metal Wheeljack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/heavy-metal-wheeljack-7f43f465554a48d3b40b3976aa658c82)
by [OrangeSauceu](https://sketchfab.com/orangesauceu), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications remove the original textures and insignia, replace the materials
with wheeljack's black, cream, and orange treatment, remove unused animations
and props, and optimize the geometry and transformation animation for the web.
The modified 3D asset remains under CC BY 4.0 and is not covered by wheeljack's
MIT license.
